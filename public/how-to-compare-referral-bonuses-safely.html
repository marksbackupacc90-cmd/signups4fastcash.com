How to compare referral bonuses safely

Referral bonuses can be a smart way to earn extra value, but they are not always worth the effort. The best offer depends on the actual reward, your eligibility, and how much work is required to unlock it. Before you click a referral link, compare the offer like a deal hunter — not just a quick clicker.

Start with the official terms

The first place to check is the merchant’s official offer page. A referral code can be valid but still come with rules about account type, funding thresholds, direct deposit requirements, region restrictions, or minimum activity.

Things to check before signing up

Look for the reward amount, any required deposit, identity verification steps, payout timing, whether you need a new account, and whether there are exclusions for existing customers. These are the details that turn a great-looking referral into a frustrating time sink.

A simple checklist

  • Is the offer still active on the official site?
  • Is the referral code for a new customer only?
  • Does the reward depend on direct deposit, purchase, or verification?
  • When does the bonus actually post, and how is it paid?
  • Are there hidden account fees or restrictions?

Red flags

Be cautious of offers that promise a large payout with vague terms, ask you to sign up for a service you do not need, or require risky funding without clear payout rules. A good referral bonus should be easy to understand, easy to verify, and fit your actual use of the product.
