How to compare referral bonuses safely

A referral bonus is only useful if you qualify and the steps make sense for you. Check what the company asks you to do, when it pays, and whether you actually want the account before using someone’s link.

Start with the official terms

The first place to check is the merchant’s official offer page. A referral code can be valid but still come with rules about account type, funding thresholds, direct deposit requirements, region restrictions, or minimum activity.

Things to check before signing up

Check the reward amount, any deposit, identity checks, payout timing, and whether the offer is for new customers only. If any of that is unclear, pause and ask the company before signing up.

A simple checklist

  • Is the offer still active on the official site?
  • Is the referral code for a new customer only?
  • Does the reward depend on a direct deposit, purchase, or identity check?
  • When does the bonus actually post, and how is it paid?
  • Are there hidden account fees or restrictions?

Red flags

Be cautious if the payout is big but the rules are vague, or if you’d have to open an account or deposit money you don’t need. Read the company’s current terms and only sign up if the offer works for you.
