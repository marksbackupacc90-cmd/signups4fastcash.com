Best free stock bonuses & brokerage welcome offers

Brokerage and investing platforms often use free stock promos to attract new users. These welcome offers can be worthwhile when the terms are clear, but it is still important to compare the real account requirements, stock funding rules, and any lock-up or transfer restrictions.

What matters most

  • Whether a deposit or transfer is required to unlock the free stock reward.
  • Whether the reward is a stock amount, cash value, or limited-time promotion.
  • Whether the app requires identity verification or a funding step.
  • How long you need to hold the account before withdrawing or transferring the value.

Typical free stock offer structure

Many platforms give a free share after you create an account and complete a simple onboarding flow, while others require a small deposit or direct transfer. The best offer is usually the one with the lowest friction and the clearest payout terms.

How to compare offers

Start with the official offer page, confirm the qualifying requirements, and then compare the same offer across multiple brokers. Don’t just compare the stock amount; look at the value of the account features and whether the reward requires extra effort beyond a quick sign-up.
