Ibotta referral bonus: how the $5 reward works

The current referral page shared with us advertises a possible $5 reward for a new Ibotta user after signup and earning cashback for the first time. The exact campaign rules can change, so review the offer inside Ibotta before completing a purchase.

New-user view: Open the referral link, create a new account, choose an eligible cashback offer, complete the required activity, and submit proof if Ibotta requests it.

How to claim the Ibotta reward

  1. Open the referral link and register as a new Ibotta user.
  2. Read the current offer details and choose a purchase you already planned to make.
  3. Activate the cashback offer before shopping and complete its requirements.
  4. Submit the receipt or connect the required account when prompted.
  5. Wait for Ibotta to confirm the cashback and referral reward.

What can affect eligibility?

Referral campaigns may be limited to new users, locations, specific stores, or a stated time period. A purchase that is returned, excluded, or completed before activating the offer may not qualify. Do not spend extra money just to chase a bonus.

How long does it take?

The reward is not necessarily instant. Ibotta may need to verify the cashback activity first. Keep your receipt and confirmation emails until the reward posts or the app explains why it did not qualify.
