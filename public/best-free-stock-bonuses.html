Best free stock and brokerage signup bonuses

Brokerage promotions are often pitched as “free stock” or “instant stock reward” offers. They can be attractive, but they usually still require account approval, a new customer account, and often some form of identity or funding verification. Compare the exact terms before you treat the reward as a guaranteed win.

Common requirements

  • New account only
  • Identity verification
  • Funding or deposit requirement
  • Account activity or eligibility window

How to compare free stock offers

Look at the value of the stock, the time it takes to land in the account, and whether the promotion is tied to a deposit or user activity. A small, easy-to-qualify offer may be better than a larger bonus with a harder requirement.
