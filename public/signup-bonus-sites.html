Best signup bonus sites & referral offers

Signup bonus sites help people compare web apps, money accounts, credit products, rewards programs, and referral deals in one place. The key is choosing offers that align with the actual terms and your real use case instead of chasing the biggest headline number.

How to evaluate a signup bonus site

  • Read the bonus requirements before creating an account.
  • Check whether a deposit, direct deposit, or purchase is required.
  • Confirm the expected payout timeline and any verification checks.
  • Compare multiple offers before choosing the best option.

Popular categories

Top signup bonus opportunities often include fintech accounts, stock apps, banking promos, cashback platforms, and high-yield money tools. Each category changes more often than a typical bank offer, so timing matters.

Use the official terms before you sign up

Referral programs can change quickly. The same code may work in one state or region but not in another, and the reward can depend on account activity. Review the merchant’s official page and compare comparable offers before you commit.
