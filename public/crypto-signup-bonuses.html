Crypto signup bonuses and referral deals

Crypto platforms and web3 apps sometimes offer signup bonuses, referral credits, or reward drops to attract new users. These offers can be attractive, but they often vary by country, account status, verification step, or the exact promo period.

Before you sign up

  • Check whether a deposit or wallet funding is required
  • Review country and age restrictions
  • Confirm the reward is awarded as cash, token credits, or points
  • Read the risks, lockups, and withdrawal terms

Best practice

Use a referral code or promo link only after comparing the offer and verifying the official terms. Crypto rewards can change quickly, and some promos are designed to draw traffic without guaranteeing value.
