Best crypto signup bonuses & referral deals

Crypto platforms sometimes offer signup bonuses, referral rewards, and web3 incentives to attract new users. These offers can be valuable, but they often vary by account type, country, verification step, and the exact campaign. Compare the terms before you risk time or funds.

What to review before joining

  • Region and age restrictions for the offer.
  • Whether the platform requires identity verification.
  • Whether the reward is actual crypto, points, or a locked bonus.
  • Any withdrawal, lockup, or trading conditions tied to the reward.

Use the official terms first

Use a referral code or promo link only after comparing the offer and verifying the official terms. Crypto rewards can change quickly, and some promos are designed to draw traffic without guaranteeing value.

How to compare offers

Look at the risk level, reward structure, and the real effort required to unlock the bonus. A smaller bonus with clear, low-friction terms may be more useful than a flashy number with hidden conditions.
