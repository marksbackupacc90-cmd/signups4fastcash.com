Apps that pay for receipts

Receipt-scanning apps may reward users with cash, points, or gift cards for submitting eligible receipts or completing other in-app tasks. They are not a way to get paid for every receipt: offers can depend on the retailer, items, purchase date, location, and submission rules. Start with purchases already in your budget and check the app’s current terms.

How receipt rewards usually work

  1. Create an account and check age, location, and new-user eligibility.
  2. Read whether an offer must be activated before shopping and which items or stores qualify.
  3. Make only a purchase you already planned, then keep the original receipt.
  4. Submit a clear, complete receipt by the stated deadline and follow any verification steps.
  5. Check approval status, the redemption minimum, available payout methods, and any restrictions.
One useful habit: take a photo of your receipt right after checkout. Faded, cropped, duplicate, or late receipts can cause a reward to be rejected.

What to compare before scanning receipts

  • Stores, products, purchase dates, and exclusions covered by an active offer
  • Receipt upload deadline, image quality, and whether digital receipts are accepted
  • Minimum redemption amount and any account verification needed to cash out
  • Whether points convert to cash, PayPal, or gift cards, and what restrictions apply
  • How rewards are reviewed and where to find the app’s current approval estimates

Protect your privacy and avoid overspending

A receipt may show where and when you shop and what you bought. Review the app’s privacy policy and permissions before submitting one, and do not upload receipts containing information the app does not need. Compare the final price with the reward, including any required purchase; a small reward is not worth buying something you do not need. Promotions and eligible products can change.

Receipt reward app FAQs

Do receipt apps pay for every receipt?

No. Some apps reward only receipts matching current offers, eligible retailers, or specific tasks. Check the rules before shopping or submitting a receipt.

How do I redeem receipt rewards?

Redemption options and minimums depend on the app and your account. Review the current cash-out terms and any identity checks in the official app before counting a reward as cash.
