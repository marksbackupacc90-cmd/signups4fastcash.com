Best cashback offers & apps

Cashback apps and rebate offers can help you recover money on everyday spending, but the best option depends on your habits. The best offer for you is the one with the combination of a low barrier to entry, a reward that matches your spending, and clear payout terms.

Important: Many cashback deals require a qualifying purchase, merchant eligibility, account verification, or a minimum order value before the reward posts.

How to compare cashback deals safely

  • Check whether the offer requires a new account or verification step.
  • Compare reward rate, payout timing, and withdrawal rules.
  • Review the allowed merchants or categories before buying.
  • Use a referral link only if you already planned to use the service.

What to watch out for

Rewards programs often update their rules for merchant exclusions, payout caps, and account verification. Before relying on a bonus, check the official offer page and read the small print carefully. A higher headline cashback rate is not always better if the reward is delayed or hard to redeem.
