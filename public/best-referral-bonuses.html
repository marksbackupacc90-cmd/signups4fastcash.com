Best referral bonuses and promo codes

Referral deals can give you extra value on fintech apps, stock accounts, cashback tools, and rewards platforms. The best offer is not always the largest headline number though — your real value depends on account requirements, payout timing, and any restrictions on who can qualify.

How to evaluate a referral deal

  • Check whether it is a new-customer offer
  • Look for identity verification and account funding rules
  • Confirm whether the reward is instant, delayed, or conditional
  • Read the official offer terms before signing up

Best referral categories

Common referral opportunities include fintech banking bonuses, stock welcome offers, cashback signups, reward apps, and crypto promotions. Each category has different reward rules, so compare the exact terms and payout method before starting.

Use the official source first

Referral bonuses are often campaign-based and can change quickly. A code or link may still be live, but the eligibility rules and reward value can vary by region, account type, and account age. The best move is to compare the current offer and verify it on the official site before funding an account.
