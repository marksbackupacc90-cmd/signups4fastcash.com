Publisher & offerwall partnerships

Signups4FastCash.com (S4FC) helps visitors compare referral and promotional offers, review available requirements, and continue to the company running an offer. We are exploring partnerships that could expand our selection to games, surveys, and other reward experiences.

Where we are today

S4FC currently publishes offer information and links visitors to providers. We record outbound offer clicks for internal analytics, but we do not currently claim to verify game milestones or guarantee that a provider will attribute a conversion. Any future offerwall or progress-tracking integration will depend on the partner's supported tools and terms.

Reward handling: The provider or network sets eligibility, approves conversions, and determines reward payment under its program terms. S4FC does not promise a reward or represent that it holds user funds.

Partnerships we are interested in

  • Publisher programs and offerwalls with games, surveys, and app offers.
  • Clear campaign requirements, eligible geographies, and user-facing reward terms.
  • Documented attribution, reporting delays, conversion statuses, and dispute procedures.
  • Supported click IDs, server-to-server postbacks, or other approved tracking options.
  • Transparent publisher approval criteria, fraud policies, payment schedules, and thresholds.

Contact us

If you represent an advertiser, affiliate network, or offerwall, email us with your program details. Please include your company and platform, offer categories and regions, publisher requirements, tracking documentation, and payment terms. We will review the fit and confirm what integration is supported before making any tracking or reward claims.
