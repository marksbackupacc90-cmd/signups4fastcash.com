Help us review it

What to include in your introduction

  • Company name, program name, and your business contact details
  • Official offer page, promotion terms, and any approved creative assets
  • Offer categories, eligible locations, and publisher approval requirements
  • Qualification steps, reward conditions, reversal rules, and payout schedule
  • Available tracking options, click identifiers, reporting, and postback documentation
  • Privacy, fraud-prevention, and brand-use requirements for publishers
  • Any restrictions on how your offer can be described or promoted

Honest measurement

Tracking that starts with a clear conversation

S4FC currently records outbound offer clicks for internal analytics. We can discuss click IDs and server-to-server postbacks where a partner supports them, but each integration must be reviewed and tested before launch.

Important: We do not currently claim to verify game milestones or guarantee that a provider will attribute a conversion. Providers and networks determine eligibility, conversion approval, and reward payment under their own terms. No listing guarantees a particular amount of traffic or conversions.
