Best cashback apps

Cashback apps may offer shopping rebates, grocery deals, gas rewards, receipt scanning, or referral bonuses, but no app rewards every purchase. The useful choice depends on participating merchants, eligible items, tracking steps, and how you can redeem rewards. Start with spending you already planned.

What makes a cashback app worth it

  • Whether your usual stores, products, or purchases are eligible
  • Activation, receipt, browser tracking, and account-linking requirements
  • Reward approval, redemption minimums, payout options, and expiration rules
  • New-user eligibility, privacy permissions, and any fees or exclusions

Best cashback apps by use case

Shopping portals or browser tools may require you to start a purchase through a tracked link; receipt apps can require an eligible item and a clear submission; location-based programs may require claiming an offer before buying. Compare the actual eligible purchase and redemption value, not just a rate shown in a promotion.

How to compare apps

Before enrolling, read how the app tracks activity and whether coupon extensions, other cashback tools, returns, or gift cards can invalidate a reward. Review its privacy policy before linking a card, retailer account, or location access. Save offer details and receipts until rewards are confirmed, and contact the provider through its official support channel if tracking fails.

Cashback app FAQs

Can I earn cashback on a purchase I already made?

It depends on the offer. Some require activation or a tracked shopping session before checkout, while others use receipt submission. Check the individual app’s current instructions; past purchases may not qualify.

Are cashback rewards guaranteed?

No. Rewards can be subject to eligibility, tracking, proof, and provider approval. Keep the confirmation and follow the official terms until the reward is approved and redeemable.
