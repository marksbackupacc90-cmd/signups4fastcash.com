Ibotta vs. Upside: which cashback app fits you?

Ibotta and Upside can both return money on everyday purchases, but they are built around different shopping habits. Use Ibotta when you want store offers and receipt-based cashback; consider Upside when you regularly buy fuel, groceries, or meals at participating locations.

Ibotta

Best for grocery and retail offers, receipt submission, and a new-user referral campaign that may reward your first cashback activity.

Typical flow: activate an offer, shop, submit proof, and wait for verification.

Upside

Best for fuel, grocery, and restaurant cashback where eligible locations are convenient for you.

Typical flow: claim an offer in the app, complete the purchase, and wait for the transaction to post.

Which should you choose?

  • Choose Ibotta if you want to compare grocery or retail offers before shopping.
  • Choose Upside if gas stations or participating restaurants are where you spend most often.
  • You can use both, but check whether an offer allows stacking before assuming you will receive two rewards.

Important terms

Rates, stores, locations, payout methods, and new-user rewards can change. Compare the current in-app terms and never make an unnecessary purchase just to earn cashback.
