Best cashback offers and rebate apps

Cashback websites and apps can help you earn money back on everyday purchases, but the best offers depend on the merchant, your shopping habits, and current promotions. Use this guide to compare signup bonuses, payout timing, and qualifying purchases before you commit.

Important: Many cashback offers require a qualifying purchase, an account approval step, or identity verification before the reward posts.

What to compare

  • Qualifying purchase amount
  • Merchant restrictions and excluded categories
  • Payout timing and withdrawal rules
  • Whether you need a browser extension or mobile app

Referral bonus apps

How to choose the right cashback offer

Start with the offers that match your spending pattern and then compare the reward rate, payout method, and time to cash out. Don’t forget to check whether the offer applies only to a specific merchant or product category.
