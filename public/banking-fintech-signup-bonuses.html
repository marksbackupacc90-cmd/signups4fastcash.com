Banking and fintech signup bonuses

Checking and savings bonuses are one of the easiest ways to earn a quick reward, but the best offers usually come with deposit, verification, or direct deposit requirements. This guide helps you compare reward amount, ease, and timeline before opening a new account.

What to check first

  • Minimum deposit or account funding requirement
  • Direct deposit, payroll, or transfer conditions
  • Identity verification requirements
  • When the bonus posts and whether it is a waiting period

Best types of fintech offers

Examples include instant cash bonuses, checking account welcome rewards, savings account boosts, and direct-deposit cash offers. Each may vary by account history, state, or account funding method.

Quick comparison rule

Don’t chase the biggest headline number if the account requires a deposit or ongoing balance you don’t want. Compare the expected value, the time to get paid, and whether the terms are realistic for your situation.
